My Butterfly Journey

This journey was quite ambitious. Call it a healing distraction or a “quest”, but the goal remained the same: to see how many of the UK's butterfly species I could find, while exploring and surveying the habitats they depend on.

That would sometimes mean travelling to distant places to seek out some of our more specialist species. That was fine — it was all part of the fun. The mission, should I choose to accept it, was on. Starting with a life list of 30 species, how much higher could I get in 2026?

Green travel

A note on carbon footprints. Living in Ely, Cambridgeshire, placed me within a couple of hours of many of the species on the 2026 agenda. Throughout this challenge, I have been conscious of trying to be a bit greener on my jaunts to more distant places. In particular, I have tried to group trips together, make use of time when we are away anyway visiting relatives, and use train journeys where I can.

However, it is not always perfect, as species tend to inhabit places that can be difficult to access. I do my best to minimise my carbon impact wherever I can.
