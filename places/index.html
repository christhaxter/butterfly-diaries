Places

From local fenland to flower-rich chalk downland, these are some of the places I've explored in search of butterflies.

Devil's Dyke, Cambridgeshire

CHALK GRASSLAND · CAMBRIDGESHIRE

Flower-rich chalk slopes and a wonderful place to look for Adonis Blues, marbled whites, dark-green fritillaries and other chalk downland butterflies.

Totternhoe, Bedfordshire

CHALK GRASSLAND · BEDFORDSHIRE

Chalk downland and old quarry workings, explored in search of grassland specialists such as the Duke of Burgundy, alongside dingy skippers, grizzled skippers and small blues.

Read the field report →

Hickling Broad, Norfolk

WETLAND · NORFOLK

Open water, reedbeds and the surrounding Broadland landscape, including the setting for a memorable swallowtail encounter.

Fermyn Woods, Northamptonshire

WOODLAND · NORTHAMPTONSHIRE

Woodland rides and clearings explored in search of elusive woodland butterflies, including the purple emperor.
