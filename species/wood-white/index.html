Wood white

Leptidea sinapis

About the species

The wood white is a delicate, slow-flying butterfly with a distinctive fluttering flight. Its slender body and elongated, rounded wings give it a particularly graceful appearance.

Identification

Wood whites are predominantly white, with subtle grey markings on the forewings. They are relatively strught-forward to identify in the field, aside from the cryptic wood white in Northern Ireland, which is ditinct in geographic range.

Habitat and distribution

In Britain, the wood white is associated with woodland rides, clearings and scrubby habitats, particularly where suitable larval foodplants grow.
